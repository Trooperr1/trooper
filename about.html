Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Since 1985

Four Decades of
Gastronomic Excellence

Le Château Rouge was founded in 1985 by the visionary Chef Pierre Beaumont, who sought to create an establishment that would redefine the art of French haute cuisine. What began as an intimate bistro has evolved into one of the world's most prestigious culinary destinations.

Our commitment to perfection earned us our first Michelin star within two years of opening. By 1995, we had achieved the coveted three-star rating, a distinction we have proudly maintained for nearly three decades. This remarkable achievement places us among the elite gastronomic temples of the world.

Today, under the masterful direction of Chef Alexandre Moreau, we continue to push the boundaries of culinary innovation while honoring the classical techniques that have defined French cuisine for centuries. Every dish that leaves our kitchen is a testament to our unwavering dedication to excellence.

Executive Chef

Chef Alexandre
Moreau

A culinary virtuoso whose innovative approach has redefined contemporary French cuisine, Chef Alexandre Moreau brings over 30 years of gastronomic expertise to Le Château Rouge. Trained under legendary masters in Lyon and Paris, Chef Moreau has earned international acclaim for his ability to transform the finest ingredients into extraordinary culinary experiences.

His philosophy centers on respect for ingredients, precision in technique, and an unwavering commitment to creating dishes that engage all the senses. Each plate is a carefully orchestrated composition where flavor, texture, and visual artistry converge in perfect harmony.
