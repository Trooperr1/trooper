OUR LEGACY

A quarter-century journey of culinary excellence, innovation, and unwavering dedication to the art of fine dining

A PASSION FOR PERFECTION

Founded in 1998 by visionary Chef Alexandre Dubois, Lumière Royale began as a dream to create the world's most extraordinary dining destination. After training under legends in Paris, earning accolades across Europe, and mastering techniques from Asia to the Americas, Chef Dubois established what would become a global icon of gastronomy.

Within three years, we earned our first Michelin star. Five years later, our second. And in 2010, we achieved the ultimate recognition: three Michelin stars, a distinction we've proudly maintained for over a decade.

What truly distinguishes Lumière Royale is our relentless pursuit of innovation while honoring timeless traditions. Every dish tells a story. Every ingredient has provenance. Every moment is memorable.

Alexandre Dubois
Chef Patron & Founder
