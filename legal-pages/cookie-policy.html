COOKIE POLICY

Cookie-Richtlinie

Last Updated: January 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Jaff Studio uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, in compliance with Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP).

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve functionality.

2. TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

2.1 ESSENTIAL COOKIES (No Consent Required)

These cookies are necessary for website operation:

  • Session management
  • Security and authentication
  • Basic functionality
  • Load balancing

Duration: Session or up to 24 hours
Legal basis: Legitimate interest (Article 31 para. 1 nFADP)

2.2 ANALYTICS COOKIES (Consent Required)

We use Google Analytics to understand website usage:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on site
  • User behavior patterns
  • Traffic sources

Duration: Up to 26 months
Legal basis: Consent (Article 31 para. 1 nFADP)
Provider: Google LLC
Data transfer: USA (adequate protection via EU-US Data Privacy Framework)
Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.3 MARKETING COOKIES (Consent Required)

Track advertising effectiveness:

  • Facebook Pixel
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag
  • Google Ads conversion tracking

Duration: Up to 12 months
Legal basis: Consent (Article 31 para. 1 nFADP)

3. HOW TO MANAGE COOKIES

3.1 Cookie Banner

When you first visit, our banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies.

3.2 Browser Settings

You can block cookies via browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies

Note: Blocking essential cookies may impair website functionality.

3.3 Opt-Out Links

  • Google Analytics: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/settings/?tab=ads
  • LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/psettings/guest-controls

4. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Hosting providers
  • Analytics platforms
  • Social media plugins

We do not control these cookies. Refer to third-party privacy policies.

5. DATA RETENTION

Cookie data is retained:

  • Essential cookies: Session or 24 hours
  • Analytics cookies: 26 months
  • Marketing cookies: 12 months

6. YOUR RIGHTS

Under Swiss law (nFADP), you have rights regarding cookie data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Cookie Policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date.
