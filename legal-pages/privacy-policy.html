2. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect the following types of personal data:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Name and contact details (email)
  • Company name and business information
  • Payment and billing information
  • Website and marketing project details
  • Communication history and correspondence

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • Usage data and analytics

2.3 Client Customer Data

When we provide services, we may process your customers' data as a data processor on your behalf, including:

  • Customer contact information
  • Marketing analytics data
  • Website visitor behavior data
  • Email marketing metrics

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

3.1 Service Delivery

  • Providing AI-powered website and marketing solutions
  • Processing payments and managing billing
  • Communicating about projects and services
  • Providing customer support

3.2 Business Operations

  • Improving our services and AI models
  • Analyzing website usage and performance
  • Marketing our services (with your consent)
  • Complying with legal obligations

3.3 AI Processing

  • We use artificial intelligence tools (including OpenAI API) to deliver our services
  • Your data may be processed by AI systems to generate marketing content, website code, and analysis
  • AI processing occurs on secure servers, primarily in the EU and Switzerland
  • We implement technical measures to ensure AI processing respects your privacy

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (nFADP)

We process your data based on:

  • Contract performance (Article 31 para. 1 nFADP)
  • Legitimate interests (Article 31 para. 1 nFADP)
  • Your consent (Article 31 para. 1 nFADP)
  • Legal obligations (Article 31 para. 2 lit. a nFADP)

5. DATA SHARING AND TRANSFERS

5.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with:

  • AI service providers (OpenAI, Anthropic)
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal)
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics)
  • Email service providers

5.2 International Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside Switzerland. We ensure adequate protection through:

  • EU-Swiss adequacy decisions
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Privacy Shield Framework (where applicable)

5.3 Legal Disclosure

We may disclose data when required by Swiss law or court order.

6. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments
  • Employee confidentiality obligations
  • Secure data backup procedures

However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. You transmit data at your own risk.

7. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal data for:

  • Active clients: Duration of business relationship + 10 years (Swiss law requirement)
  • Marketing data: Until consent withdrawal or 3 years of inactivity
  • Financial records: 10 years (Swiss accounting law)
  • Website analytics: 26 months

8. YOUR RIGHTS UNDER SWISS LAW

You have the right to:

8.1 Access (Article 25 nFADP)

Request a copy of your personal data we hold

8.2 Rectification (Article 32 para. 1 nFADP)

Correct inaccurate or incomplete data

8.3 Deletion (Article 32 para. 2 lit. a nFADP)

Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)

8.4 Restriction (Article 32 para. 2 lit. b nFADP)

Limit how we process your data

8.5 Data Portability (Article 28 nFADP)

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

8.6 Object to Processing (Article 30 para. 2 lit. b nFADP)

Object to processing based on legitimate interests

8.7 Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time (does not affect prior processing)

To exercise these rights, contact us at: contact@jaffstudio.com
