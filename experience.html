Immerse yourself in a world where every detail is meticulously crafted to perfection

Master Chefs

Our culinary team, led by Chef Patron Alexandre Dubois, features award-winning masters from the finest kitchens across Paris, Tokyo, and New York. Each dish represents decades of perfected technique and innovative vision.

🌟

Private Dining

Experience ultimate exclusivity in our private suites, each designed by renowned architects and offering personalized menus, dedicated sommeliers, and bespoke service for the most discerning clientele.

💎

Rare Ingredients

We source the world's most exceptional ingredients: white alba truffles, Osetra caviar, Japanese A5 wagyu, and rare vintage wines. Our global network ensures only the finest reach your plate.

🎭

Live Performances

Elevate your evening with intimate performances from world-class musicians, from classical virtuosos to contemporary jazz ensembles, creating the perfect ambiance for your culinary journey.

Historic Setting

Housed in a meticulously restored 18th-century palace, our dining rooms feature original frescoes, crystal chandeliers, and architectural details that transport you to an era of royal splendor.

Personalized Service

Your dedicated maître d' ensures every preference is remembered, every occasion is celebrated, and every visit exceeds expectations. Experience service elevated to an art form.
